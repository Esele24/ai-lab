07AI Voice Agent

Speak to it, it answers out loud, and when it has your name, number, date and service it writes a real booking to the database. Speech in and out is the browser's own Web Speech API; the conversation and the intent are the model's.

Said plainly: this is a browser voice agent, not a phone agent. The carousel shows inbound calls, call routing and transfers. That needs Twilio or Vapi, a purchased number and per-minute billing — none of which exists here, so none of it is claimed here. The turn logic in core/voice.py is the part that would carry over unchanged if a number were attached: swapping the transport does not change the agent.

Typing works identically — the agent does not know or care which one you used, which is the point of keeping the transport out of the agent.

Bookings taken

Written to SQLite by Python after it checks the four required fields — not by the model deciding a booking happened. That is why this agent cannot confirm a booking that does not exist.

What it is forbidden to do

  • Never invent a price. It has no price list. Asked what something costs, it collects details and says a coordinator will quote.
  • Never confirm a date is free. It cannot see a calendar.
  • Never claim to have sent an email, text or invite.
  • Replies are capped near 40 words with no markdown — a speech synthesiser reads asterisks out loud, and nobody on a call can follow a paragraph.

Browser support

Speech recognition needs Chrome or Edge. Firefox and Safari have no SpeechRecognition; there the typed input is the fallback and the agent still speaks its replies.
